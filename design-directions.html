ECE 152A. Digital Design Principles

(5) Step five on this map.

Needs. ECE 15A and ECE 10A–10AL, each with a grade of C− or better; or Computer Science 30 or 64. Open to EE, CE and CS majors only.

Unlocks. ECE 154A; and 9 electives, listed below the steps.

Counts as. Required, upper-division major.
