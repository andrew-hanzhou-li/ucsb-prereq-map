About this site

Pick a UCSB major or minor and see every course in it on one map. Each course sits to the right of the courses it needs, so you can see what to take first and what each course leads to.

How to read the map

  • Boxes are courses. An arrow from one course to another means the first one unlocks the second.
  • Left to right is order, not a schedule. Courses with no prerequisites in the program start at the left. Position doesn't mean a particular quarter, so the map works whether you started as a freshman, transferred or switched majors.
  • Click a course to see what it needs (amber) and what it unlocks (blue). The side panel gives the full prerequisites, in the catalog's own words too.
  • Some boxes hold more than one course. "One of" boxes are interchangeable courses where you take just one. Long elective lists are folded into one box; click it to see the list or unfold it onto the map.
  • Mark courses as taken and the ones you can take next get a green outline.

The colors

  • Required
  • Required, take one of the courses listed
  • Elective
  • From outside the program, needed first (off unless you tick "Prerequisites from outside")
  • Listed in the catalog but not offered now
  • A folded list of electives
  • You've marked it taken
  • You can take it next

Your marks stay on your device

Courses you mark as taken are saved in your browser on this device only. They're never sent anywhere, and the site has no accounts and no analytics. Clearing your browser's data, or using a private window, removes the marks.

Sharing a map

The address bar always names the program and the course you've selected, so you can copy it and send it. The course panel also has a "Copy a link to this course" button.

Where the data comes from

Everything comes from the UCSB General Catalog: the course list, each course's prerequisites, and each program's requirements. Course prerequisites are written in plain English in the catalog, so a program reads them into "all of" and "one of" rules. Program requirements are stored by the catalog as structured lists, so they're copied as they are.

The footer of each map names the catalog year it was built from.

What it can't check

  • Grades. "C or better" is shown, but marking a course taken assumes you met the grade.
  • "May be taken at the same time." These are labeled, but a course only counts once you mark it taken.
  • Conditions like class standing, major restrictions or instructor approval are listed, not checked.
  • Rules written as notes (for example "a maximum of 4 units of 199 courses") appear as text in the panel, not on the map.
  • Some programs have no map. The catalog doesn't list course requirements for College of Creative Studies majors, the individual major or Undeclared. They're in the lists with a note and a link to the catalog.
  • Prerequisite wording is sometimes unclear. When the catalog mixes "and" and "or" without punctuation, the panel says the reading is a best guess and shows the original wording.

This is an unofficial student project

It isn't made or endorsed by UC Santa Barbara. Requirements change, and the catalog is the official source. Before you plan around this map, confirm with your department advisor.
